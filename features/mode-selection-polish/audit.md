# ModeSelection Screen UI Audit

- **Date:** 2026-07-02
- **Build audited:** branch `feature/hero-ui-polish` @ `06aef24`, debug build on a Pixel emulator (1080×2400, API 36)
- **Scope:** `AppRoute.ModeSelection` — the study-mode picker shown after tapping a category — in both themes and both header states (baseline "Choose how you want to study" and populated mastery bar), including its shared components (`AppTile`, `TonalChip`, `SegmentedMasteryBar`, `FloatingTopBar`) and the explicit question of whether the "Night Flight" horizon background should extend to this screen
- **Method:** live screenshots captured on-device, then an 8-lens agent audit (color/theming, typography, iconography, layout, interaction/motion, accessibility, horizon feasibility, competitive benchmark) — 46 findings raised, every one then adversarially verified against source and by re-sampling pixels from the screenshots. **44 confirmed, 2 rejected**; severities below are the post-verification values. Quality bar: the sibling hero screens as left by polish steps 1–4, and best-in-class session pickers (Duolingo, Anki, Quizlet, chess.com, Headspace).
- **Evidence:** live on-device captures at 1080×2400 — the pixel coordinates quoted below refer to those originals

> File/line references are to the audited commit and will drift.

## Verdict

The screen is structurally sound — the shared `AppTile`/`TonalChip`/`FloatingTopBar` components carry the hero screens' craft down here for free, and press feedback, back navigation, the resume sheet, plural handling, and the placard chips all verify at the bar. But it visibly predates polish steps 1–4, and two issues rise above cosmetics: the four hardcoded mode accents fail the project's own 3:1 icon-on-circle floor in one theme each (the exact bug class `CategoryColors.kt` was created to fix), and card taps have zero navigation guarding — a double-tap or a fast Learning→Exam sequence pushes duplicate destinations, with a verified worst case of a hidden second `ExamViewModel` whose timer auto-finishes and stores a phantom failed result. The rest of the fix list is almost entirely mechanical ports of patterns the heroes already shipped; only the hierarchy/recommended-card questions need a product decision.

## The horizon background: partial — yes to the sky, no to the scene

**Recommendation: paint a static sky-remnant gradient at the top of the screen, inside the existing `OpaqueScreen`; do not make ModeSelection a hero route.**

**The problem is real but confined to the top quarter.** Below the header the two screens are already continuous — scene ground vs. surface measures 1.02:1 dark / 1.01:1 light, i.e. identical cards on an identical field. What the 210ms fade-through actually does is dim home's glowing sky (peak `(126,102,93)` at y≈0.175) to flat `#0A0A0C` on push and blink it back on pop — even more visibly in light theme (skyTop vs. surface 1.59:1).

**Full adoption is blocked by measured contrast, not plumbing.** The plumbing verifiably generalizes: adoption would be a 4-touchpoint diff (`isHeroRoute` in `App.kt:141`, a horizon target branch at `App.kt:95`, unwrapping `OpaqueScreen` at `AppNavGraph.kt:121`, extracting `topEdgeFade` from `CategoriesScreen.kt:437` to `ui/components`), with the cover, predictive-back, and shader-clock-pause logic needing zero changes. But:

- At the only viable height (`HOME_HORIZON_Y = 0.16`), the glow band (y≈0.15–0.20) lands exactly on the header's subtitle, mastery bar, and count line: `onSurfaceVariant` measures **2.24–2.62:1** against the band core in dark theme, and the 70%-alpha bar segments composite to ~2.18:1. Even full `onSurface` only reaches 4.4:1 at the band peak. The title zone itself is fine (8.66–6.19:1) — it's the header *body* that collides.
- Every alternative height fails differently: 0.10–0.14 puts the band behind the title (y 0.113–0.140); `LICENSE_HORIZON_Y = 0.26` peeks it around the opaque cards; and any value ≠ 0.16 makes the band visibly slide for ~700ms *after* the transition lands, because the horizon target is picked from `visibleEntries` (`App.kt:95-99`) only once the fade-through ends, then tweens for 700ms (`App.kt:232-235`) — replaying on every one-tap hop in and out of the fork screen. If adoption is ever revisited, the height must equal `HOME_HORIZON_Y` exactly; continuity comes precisely from the sky *not* moving.
- Fixing the collision means shoving the header body below y≈0.24 (~150px) or backing it with a tonal panel — both break the text-straight-on-sky language the adoption was meant to extend. There's also a dilution argument: every screen that gains the scene weakens the arrival-home moment, for a screen users see for seconds.

**The sky-remnant instead:** a static vertical gradient from `LocalHorizonPalette.current.skyTop` to `colorScheme.surface`, fully settled by y≈0.11 (the title's first text row measures y≈0.12). Measured safe in both themes — dark `onSurfaceVariant` vs. `skyTop` 8.01:1, light worst case 5.32:1 — and hue-continuous with the live scene to within 4 RGB points (scene top row `(13,14,30)` vs. `skyTop` `(14,14,34)`), so the cross-fade reads as *the sky dimming for the task ahead* rather than the world switching off. Only the back button occupies the gradient zone, and it carries its own opaque `surfaceContainer` circle. It lives entirely inside `ModeSelectionScreen` behind the existing `OpaqueScreen`, costs no shader time, and preserves the scene's semantics: **live sky = home base, sky remnant = fork in the road, opaque = task.**

Secondary risks of full adoption were checked and are all manageable (shader clock is ~30Hz-capped and pauses under cover; disabled cards stay opaque; the resume sheet's scrim is the same overlay class as home's dropdown) — recorded here so a future revisit doesn't re-litigate them. The go/no-go fact is the header-over-band contrast.

## What's already at the bar (keep)

- **Press feel is structurally identical to the hero tiles** — one `MutableInteractionSource` wired into both the ripple and `rememberPressScale` (60ms grab, low-bouncy spring release) via the shared `AppTile`.
- **ResumePromptDialog** — correct emphasis order (filled Continue over tonal Start over), safe no-op scrim dismiss, warm copy, both action handlers re-entrancy-guarded.
- **Back navigation** — arrow, system back, and predictive back are consistent; pop transitions mirror forward; `OpaqueScreen` guards predictive-back previews; return-to-home correctly skips the entrance replay.
- **The five-card full-screen pattern earns its place** — this is the subject's landing page with a per-subject mastery dashboard and rich per-mode state (live counts, durations, disabled-state education); a Quizlet-style sheet or segmented control couldn't express it. Invest in hierarchy within the pattern, not a lighter picker.
- **Cockpit honesty beats the benchmarks' noise** — true inventory in B612 Mono placards ("608 questions", "28 questions · 42 min"), education where leaders put paywalls, zero gamification clutter. Preserve as a hard constraint on any "recommended" treatment: badges must state data, never manufactured urgency.
- **Merged card semantics, disabled announcements, touch targets, reduced motion** — each card is one focus target read in sensible order; `enabled=false` announces disabled while hints stay readable; back button 48dp; both bars snap under reduced motion; the silent Canvas bar is fully covered by the header's single-phrase description (same treatment as home's tiles).
- **Chip and count-line craft** — placards 5.32:1 dark / 6.87:1 light; count-line words 8.46:1 with numbers 15.88:1; PL strings length-balanced with full one/few/many plural forms kept number-free so styled counts inflect correctly; no truncation risk (descriptions un-clamped, cards grow).
- **Icon family discipline** — all five glyphs Outlined matching the hero grid, `MenuBook` correctly auto-mirrored, five distinct silhouettes tellable apart at a glance, and each metaphor reuses an association the app already taught (bookmark = save toggle, timer = exam timer, circle-X + red = incorrect).
- **The baseline hint's typographic voice** — bodyLarge subtitle under the headline is the app's established page-subtitle grammar (LicenseSelection uses the identical pattern); its progressive swap-out for the mastery bar is good behavior. (A restyle toward `HomeSectionHeader` was proposed and refuted in verification — it would *create* an inconsistency.)

## High-severity findings

### H1. Four hardcoded mode accents are theme-blind — the exact bug class CategoryColors.kt just fixed one screen up

`ModeSelectionScreen.kt:111/120/131/149` hardcode Learning `#1E88E5`, Quick review `#00897B`, Exam `#5C6BC0`, Saved `#E0A100` — one hex serving both themes for both the glyph tint and the tonal circle. Computed icon-vs-circle contrast: Learning **2.61:1 light** (3.67 dark); Quick review 2.99:1 light (borderline); Exam **2.88:1 dark** — fails in the app's primary theme (3.40 light); Saved 5.43:1 dark but **1.71:1 light**. Each color loses in one theme. `CategoryColors.kt`'s own kdoc rejects this approach verbatim ("a single fixed hex … reads muddy in one theme or the other"), and the one theme-aware icon on this screen — My mistakes via `LocalQuizColors.incorrect` — measures 4.98:1 dark / 3.03:1 light, proving the token approach clears the bar on the same construction. The four hexes have no other consumers.

**Fix:** mirror the hero fix — a themed `ModeColors` (or extended `CategoryColors`) with light/dark pairs per mode, provided via the theme like `Light`/`DarkCategoryColors`, deleting the four hexes from `learningModes()`. Target the hero bar's floors: ≥3.5:1 light, ~6.7:1 dark. Priority pairs: Saved gold in light (1.71:1) and Exam indigo in dark (2.88:1). See M8 for choosing the hues against the category palette.

### H2. Unguarded card taps can double-navigate — worst case is a phantom failed exam result

`onModeSelected` (`ModeSelectionViewModel.kt:98-123`) has no in-flight guard: sync modes (Exam, Saved, My mistakes) navigate on every tap, and Learning/Quick review launch a fresh coroutine per tap that awaits a DataStore read before navigating. None of the app's 20 `navigate()` calls use `launchSingleTop` or lifecycle guards, and the outgoing screen keeps receiving pointer events through the ~300ms fade-through window. A double-tap pushes two destinations. Verification proved the consequence *stronger* than first claimed for Exam: each duplicate `AppRoute.Exam` entry owns an `ExamViewModel` that starts in `init` and whose timer auto-runs `validateAndFinish()` at expiry — the hidden duplicate stores a phantom 0-correct failed `SessionResult` if the app outlives it, which is plausible since the user is actively sitting the visible exam for exactly that duration. (Answer-log/mastery stats stay safe — empty answer sets early-return.) The fast Learning→Exam cross-mode race is mechanically real but narrow (warm DataStore reads are fast). The resume-sheet buttons are already correctly guarded (`:125-143`) — only card taps are exposed.

**Fix:** wrap the nav callbacks with `dropUnlessResumed` (lifecycle-runtime-compose 2.9.6 is already in the version catalog; the API is in the KMP artifact since 2.8.0) plus `launchSingleTop` on the calls; apply app-wide, ModeSelection first. Prefer this over a VM in-flight flag — the VM has no resume signal today, so a flag would need extra plumbing to reset.

## Medium findings

### Disabled-state legibility

- **M1. Disabled-card double-dimming degrades the app's only feature education** *(merges four lenses' findings; not a WCAG compliance failure — 1.4.3 exempts inactive controls — but a verified design-intent failure)*. `AppTile.kt:63` stacks `contentAlpha = 0.5` on already-muted colors: hint copy measures **2.37:1 light / 2.75:1 dark** (enabled equivalent 7.67/6.94:1); disabled titles 3.17:1 light / 4.42:1 dark. The glyph dims separately — a 0.38-alpha accent whose gold bookmark measures **1.24:1 vs. its circle in light**, an empty circle. (The circle fill itself is *unchanged* from enabled cards — the 16% tint replaces, not stacks, the 0.38.) These hints are the app's only unlock instructions, shipped specifically as a discoverability fix (design §7, PR #20 follow-up), and the first-run state shows both cards disabled simultaneously — exactly the user who must read them. TalkBack is unaffected (visual-only).
  **Fix:** exempt the description from `contentAlpha` — ModeSelection is the *only* `AppTile` call site passing `enabled=false`, so the change has zero blast radius; full `onSurfaceVariant` measures 6.9–7.7:1. For glyphs, use `onSurface.copy(alpha = 0.38f)` on a neutral circle (M3 convention) or per-theme disabled tints — gold at 38% over a pale surface can never read. Keep title dimming so the disabled state survives.

### Header mastery bar

- **M2. Bar segments at 0.70 alpha fail 3:1 in light theme — the identical trap AppTile fixed and documented.** `ModeSelectionScreen.kt:302-303` passes 0.70-alpha fills unconditionally: mastered 2.73:1, to-review 2.40:1 vs. the light track (screenshot-verified 2.41:1); dark passes (4.14/3.18). `AppTile.kt:136-139` documents and gates this exact trap (*"drops below the 3:1 non-text minimum on the light track, so the light fill runs fully opaque"*); the header bar shipped without the gate. Severity medium rather than high because the count line and the a11y phrase fully duplicate the bar's information — nothing is lost to any user — but it remains a measured violation of the project's own documented standard for this exact pattern, and a cross-screen inconsistency with the home tiles' bars.
  **Fix:** the same `LocalIsDarkTheme` gate (0.70 dark / 1f light — full-opacity light verifies 4.59/3.33:1 and improves adjacent-segment separation 1.14→1.38:1), ideally moved *inside* `SegmentedMasteryBar` so no caller can regress; it has one production caller.
- **M3. The bar's documented 350ms entrance fill never plays.** `animateFloatAsState` initializes *at* its first target, and the bar is only ever composed with final fractions (fresh composition on arrival, on header-state switch, and on pop-back), so no target ever changes while composed. The kdoc at `SegmentedMasteryBar.kt:21-22` describes behavior that does not occur; `AppTile`'s `TileProgressBar` shares the same dead pattern — so home's tile bars are silently static too. **Fix:** an explicit 0→1 entrance progress (remembered `Animatable(0f)` + `LaunchedEffect`, reusing the reduced-motion snap, gated like `Entrance.kt` so pop-backs don't replay), fixed in the shared components; correct the stale doc comments in all three places.

### Motion & structure

- **M4. No entrance choreography.** Both hero neighbours stagger in via the shared `Entrance.kt`; this screen pops in as one static block after the fade-through, and its loading state is a bare spinner vs. home's shimmer skeletons. **Fix:** reuse the exact hero recipe — `rememberSaveable` entrance gate, index 0 on the header, 1..5 on the cards (~550ms total at home's 40ms cadence); optionally shimmer silhouettes for loading.
- **M5. Scroll shear at an invisible hard clip below the floating bar** — the same defect home fixed. The scroll column clips at its own top bound 8dp under the back circle; no surface marks the line. Doesn't scroll at default font, but does at font scale ≥~1.3, on small phones, and in landscape (reachable on both platforms). **Fix:** extract `topEdgeFade` from `CategoriesScreen.kt` into `ui/components`, apply with `top = TOP_EDGE_FADE + 8.dp` content padding — also a prerequisite for the sky-remnant.
- **M6. The disabled tail ends the fresh screen on ~200dp of dead grey**, 40% of the first-visit list, and once practised a grey Saved card sits sandwiched between two live ones. **Fix:** split into two labelled sections — study modes vs. collections — reusing `HomeSectionHeader`; a section boundary makes a grey collection card read as "not started yet" rather than a dead action. Optionally compact empty-collection cards (title + hint, no reserved chip row).

### Type & copy

- **M7. Header count-line numerals aren't B612 Mono** — "2" and "655" render in default sans bold (`appendCountToken` sets only weight) while every other number on screen is in the instrument face. The most prominent numerals on the screen are the only ones off-convention. **Fix:** add `fontFamily = LocalAppFonts.current.numeric` to the number `SpanStyle`.
- **M8. Quick review's description says nothing** — "A short round of questions from this category": "short" is the chip's job, "from this category" is true of every card, and the copy hides the mode's actual differentiator (weighted picks: unseen → wrong → old-correct, per `ShortLearningQuestionStrategy`'s kdoc). **Fix:** state the value, e.g. "A quick session focused on what you haven't mastered yet" (PL impersonal form avoids gendered past tense: "Krótka sesja z pytań, które warto powtórzyć"); keep ≤~46 chars for one line.

### Color system

- **M9. Mode accents are drawn from the category palette's hue space without coordination.** Red is Air Law's identity on home; one tap later red means "incorrect" (mistakes card + bar segment) under the same "Air Law" title. Learning's blue nearly duplicates Flight Planning's; Saved's gold sits on Human Performance's amber; Exam's indigo shadows both Operational Procedures and the brand primary. Within the screen the semantics are coherent and deliberately reasoned — the gap is only that the mode palette was picked ad hoc from hues the category system already owns. **Fix:** when building the themed `ModeColors` (H1), keep the two genuinely semantic accents (gold bookmark, `incorrect` red) and consider unifying Learning/Quick review/Exam on one or two brand tones; settle red-as-Air-Law vs. red-as-incorrect once, in `CategoryColors`, even if the answer is "accept it".

### Accessibility

- **M10. No heading semantics in either header state** (hero screens set `heading()` on their headers). Worse, the populated header's `clearAndSetSemantics` folds the title into one plain-text node, so TalkBack heading navigation finds nothing on the screen. **Fix:** `heading()` on the title in `MasteryHeaderColumn`; scope `clearAndSetSemantics` to a sub-column wrapping only the bar + count line (dropping the title prefix from the phrase), restoring the title as its own heading node while keeping the single-phrase count summary.

## Low findings

- **L1. Card titles render B612 Regular** — `Typography.kt:59` pins `titleMedium`'s family but not weight; B612 has only 400/700 cuts, the Medium(500) request resolves down to 400 (verified against the font matcher and empirically: 4px median stroke = the regular cut), and synthesis only fakes bold ≥600. Verification reframed this from a ModeSelection gap to an **app-wide type-system gap**: the LicenseSelection hero pairs title+description identically, so the heroes render the same way, and hierarchy still has two cues (size, B612-vs-sans family) — just not weight. The fix also has a wide blast radius (`titleMedium` feeds 10+ sites: quiz top bar, sheets, results, preview). **Fix:** pin `fontWeight` deliberately in `appTypography` (or register the bold file at `FontWeight.Medium`) as its own app-wide sweep with visual review of all title-slot consumers — not as part of this screen's polish.
- **L2. Hierarchy is flat while a code comment calls Exam "the headline action".** Five near-identical tiles; Exam sits third and ties Quick review for height (~127.6/107.4/107.4/97.5/97.5dp). Verification tempered it: the brand-indigo accent *is* the intended differentiation (it just fails since every card has a distinct hue), Exam is the only card with two chips, all five fit one viewport, and equal-weight options are a legitimate chooser pattern — an internal-consistency polish gap, not a usability failure. **Fix if desired:** a differentiated Exam container (accent tonal fill, or an accent outline *replacing* the light-theme hairline, not stacking on it). Goal-first reordering is a product call. Pairs with L9.
- **L3. Disabled Exam card keeps its invitation copy** — greyed, dead-tapping, "Test yourself under real exam conditions", no explanation (no disabled-exam string exists in either language). Saved/Mistakes swap in self-explaining empty copy; Exam should too, or hide when duration metadata is missing.
- **L4. Three of five EN descriptions wrap with 1–2-word orphans** ("…at your / own pace", "…to collect them / here"), giving an uneven 2/1/1/2/2-line card rhythm. Trim to the ~46–48-char one-line budget; PL equivalents are also over.
- **L5. Mastery bar is horizontally asymmetric** — 20dp left / 16dp right (header column pads `start = 4.dp` only). Add `end = 4.dp`.
- **L6. No width adaptation in landscape** — full-bleed ~768dp cards vs. home's `GridCells.Adaptive(300.dp)`; landscape is reachable on both platforms. Constrain content to `widthIn(max ≈ 480.dp)` centered.
- **L7. Mode cards expose no `Role.Button`** — M3 `Surface(onClick)` passes no role; consistent with home (shared component) but below the hero polish's new roles bar. One-line fix in `AppTile` benefits both screens.
- **L8. Exam card's chip Row overflows at 2× font scale** — plain `Row`, no wrapping; the "42 min" pill deforms. `FlowRow` + optional `maxLines = 1` guard on `TonalChip`.
- **L9. Icon nits:** the `ModeSelectionScreen.kt:167` comment claims the mistakes circle-X is "the same fail glyph the results screen uses" — results actually uses the *filled* variant, so the continuity is hue+shape, not glyph (works anyway when populated; weakest on the dimmed empty card, where a red X on grey scans briefly as "error"). `MenuBook` does double duty (Learning mode vs. explanation Source row) — differentiate only if either surface is revisited (`Outlined.School` is the natural Learning upgrade). Timer and Cancel are both ring-shaped at 24dp and lean on inner mark + hue.
- **L10. No recommended/primary action:** all five cards weigh the same although the ViewModel already computes everything a recommender needs (mastery buckets, mistakes count) — the data-backed next action ("My mistakes — 2 to review") is the shortest card and dead last, while leaders (Duolingo, chess.com, Quizlet) make the recommended session dominant. A pure, testable rule exists: `toReview > 0 → My mistakes; seenCount == 0 → Quick review; remaining == 0 → Exam`. Placard-honest badge only ("2 to review", never urgency). Product call.
- **L11. Zero-progress state gives no first-session steer:** nothing signals that 10-question Quick review is the low-commitment first tap vs. 608-question Learning, and 33% of the first-visit stack is disabled content. A one-line steer or a "good first step — 10 questions" badge; keep the disabled cards' education.

## Rejected on verification (do not re-raise)

- **"Header presents inventory, not interpretation / '655 remaining' is demotivating"** — the minimalist bar + raw count line is a documented owner decision (mockup variants A–D were compared; "Variant A, minimalist — no legend/%/sentence; colour IS the legend" was chosen deliberately, and the count line was further quieted in a later color audit). If readiness framing ever comes to this screen, it arrives via the product-layer work (flight-deck card), not by re-opening the header.
- **"The baseline hint uses a different typographic voice than home's section header"** — refuted: the app has one consistent grammar *per role*. bodyLarge-subtitle-under-title is exactly how the just-polished LicenseSelection renders the same construct; home's "Choose a category" is a *section label* in a two-section grid (a different M3 role, correctly styled differently). Restyling the hint to `HomeSectionHeader` would break parity with LicenseSelection — the stated quality bar.

## Suggested implementation order

Fits the existing `feature/hero-ui-polish` step cadence; steps are independently shippable.

1. **Tokens & contrast** (H1 + M1 + M2, with M9's hue decisions): themed `ModeColors`, disabled de-dimming in `AppTile`, bar alpha gate in `SegmentedMasteryBar`. Mirrors hero step 1.
2. **Navigation guard** (H2): `dropUnlessResumed` + `launchSingleTop` app-wide, ModeSelection first. Small, self-contained, de-risks the most-tapped path.
3. **Motion & scene** (M3 + M4 + M5 + horizon partial): entrance stagger, real bar fill in the shared components, `topEdgeFade` extraction, sky-remnant gradient. Mirrors hero steps 3–4.
4. **Copy & numerals** (M7 + M8, L3 + L4): mono count-line, Quick-review rewrite, disabled-Exam string, one-line trims.
5. **Hierarchy & product layer** (M6, L2 + L10 + L11): sections, Exam prominence, recommended-action rule — needs a product decision first; coordinate with the home flight-deck card so the two "what next" signals agree.
6. **A11y & fit-and-finish sweep** (M10, L5–L9). The B612 weight pinning (L1) is its own app-wide sweep with visual review of every title-slot consumer.
